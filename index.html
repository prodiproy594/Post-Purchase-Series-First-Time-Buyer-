The Care Guide

Engineered to Last • Maintained with Ease

Your Schatzii gear is built for effortless daily durability. Here are a few quick tips to keep your Smart Cloths and hardware performing like brand new.

01 • Effortless Smart Cloth Cleaning
Machine Washable & Machine Dryable

Whenever your Smart Cloth picks up dust, oils, or everyday smudges, simply toss it in the laundry! Schatzii Smart Cloths are both machine washable and machine dryable—making cleanup completely hassle-free so you can reuse them endlessly.

02 • 100% Spray-Free Routine
Protect Screen Coatings

Skip harsh alcohol or ammonia sprays on Retina, OLED, or anti-reflective lenses. Use the plush side of your Smart Cloth dry to lift away grime, then buff with the smooth side for streak-free optical clarity.

03 • Battery & Audio Longevity
Charging Capsule Care

For the Bullet 2.0 and portable batteries, avoid leaving charging capsules in direct extreme heat and keep the magnetic contact points free of pocket lint for reliable charging every time.
