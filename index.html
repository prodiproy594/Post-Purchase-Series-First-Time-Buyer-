The Care Guide

Engineered to Last. Maintained with Ease.

Your Schatzii gear is designed for daily durability. Here are a few essential tips to keep your screen cloths and hardware operating at peak optical precision for years to come.

01 • Smart Cloth Washing
Cold Water & Air Dry

Whenever your Smart Cloth accumulates dust or oils, hand wash or machine wash in cold water with mild detergent. Always air dry. Do not use fabric softeners or heat drying, as heat breaks down the microscopic electrostatic weave.

02 • 100% Spray-Free Routine
Protect Screen Coatings

Never spray alcohol or ammonia solutions directly onto Retina, OLED, or anti-reflective lenses. Use the plush side of your Smart Cloth dry to lift grime, then buff with the smooth side for crystal clarity.

03 • Battery & Audio Longevity
Charging Capsule Care

For the Bullet 2.0 and portable batteries, avoid storing charging capsules in direct extreme heat. Keep magnetic contact points free from pocket lint for instant charging every time.
